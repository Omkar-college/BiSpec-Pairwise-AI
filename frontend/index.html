🏆 Why ROC-AUC & PR-AUC are the Golden Discovery Metrics

Addressing academic evaluation: Why traditional accuracy is misleading in high-stakes oncology target discovery.

1. Extreme Class Imbalance Invariance (Less than 0.1% Viable Pairs) ▼
Across the 12.5 million possible cell-surface antigen pairs in the human proteome, fewer than 0.05% represent clinically safe and pharmacologically synergistic pairs. If a trivial baseline classifier simply predicts "Negative" for every single candidate, it achieves 99.95% Accuracy while curing zero patients. ROC-AUC evaluates the true positive rate against the false positive rate across all decision thresholds, remaining robust to severe class imbalance.
2. Threshold-Independent Pairwise Ranking Fidelity ▼
In pharmaceutical R&D, scientists do not need a rigid binary cutoff at 0.5; they need to prioritize top candidate pairs for experimental synthesis in descending order. Mathematically, the ROC-AUC is equivalent to the Wilcoxon-Mann-Whitney U statistic: the exact probability that a randomly selected clinically approved/viable pair will be scored higher by the model than a randomly selected discontinued/toxic pair.
3. Precision-Recall AUC (PR-AUC) Synergy for Wet-Lab Costs ▼
Synthesizing, purifying, and testing a single bispecific antibody in animal models costs $50,000 to $100,000+ and months of laboratory effort. Therefore, false positives are prohibitively expensive. PR-AUC directly measures precision over the minority class, ensuring that when the AI nominates a high-scoring pair, the probability of false-positive laboratory failure is minimized.
4. Oncological Safety Asymmetry & Zero-False-Positive Tuning ▼
A false positive in oncology therapeutics can cause lethal Cytokine Release Syndrome (CRS) or auto-reactivity against vital healthy organs (e.g. heart, lungs, liver). The ROC curve allows immunologists to visualize and select an operational threshold operating at high specificity (near-zero false positive rate), safeguarding clinical patient safety before lead optimization.

🎓 Evaluator Explanatory Guide • Project Milestone 1

Simplified plain-English analogies and rigorous biological definitions for all technical aspects of BiSpec Pairwise AI.

1. What is a Bispecific Antibody (BsAb)?
💡 Plain-English Analogy (For Evaluator): "Molecular Handcuffs." Traditional cancer drugs are single keys that lock one front door; cancer quickly adapts and escapes through the back window. A bispecific antibody has two distinct binding hands: it either cuffs a cancer cell directly to an immune killer T-cell (dragging the assassin to the target), or it simultaneously padlocks both the front door and the back door of the tumor so it cannot mutate around the treatment.
🔬 Technical Biological Definition: An artificial monoclonal antibody engineered with two non-identical antigen-binding sites (Fab or scFv arms). Major modalities include T-cell redirection (BiTEs: e.g. CD3 + CD19/CLDN18) and dual-pathway receptor tyrosine kinase blockade (e.g. EGFR + MET) to suppress resistance.
2. The 12.5 Million Combinatorial Explosion & Pairwise Learning
💡 Plain-English Analogy (For Evaluator): "Finding the Needle in a 12.5-Million Haystack." Testing pairs one by one in mice or test tubes would cost billions of dollars and take decades. Pairwise AI acts as a computational screening funnel—ranking and sieving through millions of possible drug pairs in seconds to nominate the safest, most effective candidates for laboratory testing.
🔬 Technical Biological Definition: Given approximately 5,000 cell-surface membrane proteins, all possible non-redundant pairs equal $N(N-1)/2 \approx 12.5\times 10^6$. Our pairwise engine constructs symmetric feature vectors capturing joint expression, cell co-localization, and normal tissue safety floors.
3. Permutation Invariance ($A + B \equiv B + A$)
💡 Plain-English Analogy (For Evaluator): "Commutative Property in Medicine." Whether you say "Aspirin + Caffeine" or "Caffeine + Aspirin", they are the exact same pill in the human body. Our AI guarantees that switching the order of the two targets produces the exact same score, preventing model hallucination or order bias.
🔬 Technical Biological Definition: The scoring function $f(\mathbf{x}_A, \mathbf{x}_B) = f(\mathbf{x}_B, \mathbf{x}_A)$ is mathematically invariant under input permutation, achieved through symmetric pairwise feature engineering (minimums, products, and unordered cosine similarities).
4. Single-Cell RNA Sequencing vs. Bulk Sequencing
💡 Plain-English Analogy (For Evaluator): "Fruit Salad vs. Fruit Smoothie." Bulk RNA sequencing blends millions of patient cells into a smoothie; you can't tell whether sweetness came from strawberries or rotten apples. Single-cell sequencing inspects every individual cell separately, ensuring we only target cells that are truly malignant while sparing healthy normal cells.
🔬 Technical Biological Definition: High-throughput transcriptomic profiling across 208,506 individual cells from NSCLC tumors, lymph nodes, and paired non-malignant lung tissues (GSE131907), resolving distinct epithelial, T/NK, myeloid, endothelial, and stromal cell populations.
5. Normal Tissue Safety Floor (Min Safety Score)
💡 Plain-English Analogy (For Evaluator): "The Weakest Link Circuit Breaker." A bispecific drug might have one target that destroys cancer, but if the second target is also expressed in the patient's heart or lungs, the drug will cause fatal organ damage. The Safety Floor ensures that both targets clear strict safety thresholds before the AI gives a green light.
🔬 Technical Biological Definition: Quantifies $\min(\text{Safety}_A, \text{Safety}_B)$ where safety indices are computed from background expression in healthy normal tissues. This explicitly penalizes on-target off-tumor toxicity liabilities.
6. Biological Synergy & Pathway Convergence
💡 Plain-English Analogy (For Evaluator): "Cutting Off Both Power Generators." When an oncologist attacks cancer with one inhibitor, the cancer cell switches on an auxiliary power generator to survive. Biological synergy means the second arm of our bispecific antibody unplugs the auxiliary generator simultaneously, causing synthetic lethality.
🔬 Technical Biological Definition: Convergence on critical oncogenic signaling pathways (MAPK, PI3K/AKT, JAK/STAT) or simultaneous inhibition of immune checkpoints (e.g. PD-L1 + 4-1BB) to overcome microenvironmental exhaustion.
7. Why ROC-AUC & PR-AUC Instead of Standard Accuracy?
💡 Plain-English Analogy (For Evaluator): "The 99.9% Illusion." If 999 out of 1,000 target combinations fail in medicine, a lazy computer program that always says "No" achieves 99.9% accuracy—yet discovers zero cures! ROC-AUC and PR-AUC measure the AI's actual ability to rank rare winning therapies above failures without being fooled by the ocean of negatives.
🔬 Technical Biological Definition: In severe class imbalance ($< 0.1\%$ positive pairs), Accuracy is degenerate. ROC-AUC mathematically equals the Wilcoxon-Mann-Whitney ranking probability, and PR-AUC directly optimizes precision over the minority clinical class to protect wet-lab budgets.
8. Novel / Uncharacterized vs. Clinically Documented Combinations
💡 Plain-English Analogy (For Evaluator): "Known Roads vs. New Goldmines." If a target pair already has clinical trials (like CD274 + TNFRSF9), our system cross-references its clinical trial phase. If a target pair has high synergy but no existing drugs (like ERBB2 + IL17F), our system highlights it as an "Uncharacterized Novel Discovery" ripe for patenting and preclinical laboratory nomination.
🔬 Technical Biological Definition: Automated cross-referencing against our 791-record clinical bispecific registry, distinguishing known investigational combinations from novel, patentable target pairings.
